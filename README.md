A dog explains, in one line, why it ate the homework: it checked the maths, found three mistakes, and ate the evidence before the teacher could mark it — a public service, payable in bacon.

> I checked your maths, found three mistakes, and ate the evidence before your teacher could mark it. You're welcome. I accept payment in bacon.
